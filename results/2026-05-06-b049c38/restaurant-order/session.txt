The key insight: mt-auto only works when the parent has a defined height, and CardLayout doesn't expose that. Instead, grid-rows-[auto_auto_1fr_auto] works independently of parent height — the 1fr row (description) absorbs all the extra space, which pushes the last row (price + button) to the bottom of whatever height the tallest card in the row sets. No dependency on the parent's height at all.
